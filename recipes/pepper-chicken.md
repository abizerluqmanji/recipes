# Pepper Chicken

**Author**: Anila

Fun and easy pepper chicken to eat with rice or roti.  
5 servings, 10 minute prep time, 25 minute cook time.

## Ingredients

- 400g Chicken, cut to desired size
- 2 tbsp Avocado/Canola oil
- 2 Onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, or 3 cloves of Garlic and 2 cubes of Frozen Ginger (or 1-inch fresh Ginger)
- 2 Tomatoes, diced or crushed
- 2 tsp Chilli Powder
- 2 tsp Coriander Powder
- 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
- 2 tsp Garam Masala
- A loooooooot of Crushed Pepper
- 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
- 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
- 1/2 cup Coriander Leaves
- Lemon Juice, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Water

## Directions

1. Marinate the chicken in salt and lemon juice.
2. Heat oil in a saucepan at medium-high. Once hot enough, add mustard seeds and wait until they crackle. Then add curry leaves and wait for the spatter to calm down.
3. Add sliced onions to the pan with a little salt and let them sweat (3-5 mins). Once the onions are translucent, add the ginger-garlic paste and sautée. Once the raw smell disappears (2-4 mins), add the tomatoes.
4. Add all the powders to the dish and sautée for an additional two minutes. Adjust the heat so as to not burn anything.
5. Add a lot of crushed pepper (at least 1 tbsp), and salt. Add lemon juice to desired tartness.
6. Add the chicken and two cups of water, and let it boil on a medium-low flame (10-15 minutes). If there's too little water, add more.
7. Add more pepper. Taste check!
8. Once desired consistency is reached, add coriander leaves. Cook for 1-2 minutes, then shut off the flame.
