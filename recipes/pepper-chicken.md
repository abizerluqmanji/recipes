# Pepper Chicken

**Author**: Anila

Fun and easy pepper chicken to eat with rice or roti.  
5 servings, 10 minute prep time, 25 minute cook time.

## Ingredients

- 400g Chicken breast, cubed
- 2 tbsp Avocado/Canola oil
- 2 Onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste, or 3 cloves of Garlic and 2 cubes of Frozen Ginger (or 1-inch fresh Ginger)
- 2 Tomatoes, diced or crushed
- 2 tsp Chilli Powder
- 2 tsp Coriander Powder
- 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
- 2 tsp Garam Masala
- A loooooooot of Crushed Pepper
- 2 sprigs Curry Leaves
- 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
- 1/2 cup Coriander Leaves
- Lemon Juice, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Water

## Directions

1. Marinate the chicken in salt and lemon juice.
1. Heat oil in a saucepan on medium-high heat.
1. Once the oil is hot, add mustard seeds and wait until they crackle.
1. Add curry leaves and wait for the spatter to calm down.
1. Add sliced onions to the pan with a little salt and let them sweat for 3-5 minutes.
1. Once the onions are translucent, add the ginger garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears (2-4 minutes).
1. Add the tomatoes.
1. Add all the powders to the dish and sauté for an additional two minutes. Adjust the heat to avoid burning.
1. Add a lot of crushed pepper (at least 1 tbsp) and salt. Add lemon juice to desired tartness.
1. Add the chicken and cook covered on medium-low heat for 5 minutes.
1. Add one cup of water and cook on medium-low heat for 10 minutes. Add more water if needed.
1. Add more pepper. Taste and adjust seasoning.
1. Once desired consistency is reached, add coriander leaves. Cook for 1-2 minutes, then shut off the heat.
